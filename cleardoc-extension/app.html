    upload_file How to Upload

    Tap the upload area on the Home or Upload tab to select a PDF, DOCX, or image file. You can also use the camera to scan physical documents.

    translate Languages

    Select your preferred output language before simplifying. We support English, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, Malayalam, Urdu, and many more.

    history History

    Save simplified documents to your history for quick access later. Tap any saved item to view the full explanation.

    task_alt Checklist
